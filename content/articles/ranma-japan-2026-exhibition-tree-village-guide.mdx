## How to Buy the Exhibition Ticket From Overseas
Lawson Ticket (l-tike.com) runs the primary allocation for the Ranma 1/2 Exhibition. The Japanese-language site is the one to use — the English-language l-tike.com/en page does not currently carry the Ranma exhibition listing, so plan to navigate the Japanese site with a browser translation plugin.
1. Go to **l-tike.com** and search for **らんま** (Ranma) or **らんま1/2展** (Ranma 1/2 Exhibition).
2. Select the event, then choose a specific date and time-slot entry window (typical two-hour slots).
3. Select either the **一般チケット** (General Ticket, 2,000 yen advance) or **特典付きチケット** (Bonus Ticket, 5,000 yen advance, includes the 3D acrylic standee).
4. Complete checkout. A Japanese phone number is not required — a confirmation email with a QR code is sent to any email address and is redeemable at the Sunshine City front counter on the day.
5. Arrive at Sunshine City Exhibition Hall A at least 15 minutes before your entry window. Show the QR code at the exhibition entrance.
Day-of tickets are 2,200 yen general and 5,200 yen bonus, subject to availability. The bonus-ticket 3D acrylic standee sells out on the advance-ticket side for opening week — if you want the standee, book advance through Lawson Ticket before you land.

## Tree Village Flower Bouquet Pop-Up: How the Cafe Works
Tree Village's POP UP SHOP & CAFE format at Tokyo Skytree Town Solamachi combines a walk-in merchandise store with a small cafe counter. The cafe section requires a food or drink order per seat, and a paid order triggers a random-draw novelty — typically a coaster or postcard featuring the flower-bouquet key visual.
The **flower bouquet theme for 2026** features newly drawn illustrations of the main cast holding or surrounded by seasonal bouquets, with each character assigned a bouquet reflecting their personality — a Shampoo amaryllis bouquet, an Akane wild-iris and lily arrangement, a Kodachi black-rose spray. These illustrations debut on Tree Village merchandise, including acrylic stands, canvas art boards, tote bags, and postcards.
Menu items are not yet published (expected to drop April 28, per the Tree Village announcement pattern), but based on previous Tree Village pop-up collaborations, expect four to six themed drinks and plates ranging from 1,200 to 1,800 yen each, plus a one-order-per-seat minimum during the reservation-required windows.

## The Sunshine City Exhibition: What You Are Seeing
The exhibition runs three hours of self-paced content across Exhibition Hall A, themed around five zones:
1. **Tendo Family Living Room Recreation** — a full-scale physical set of the Tendo dojo's living room where visitors pose for photos alongside Ranma and Akane standees. The press-day imagery showed a one-to-one-scale kotatsu, sliding shoji doors, and period-correct early-1990s Japanese household details.
2. **Original Storyboard and Setting-Art Gallery** — archival cel setups, character model sheets, and original background art drawn by Fuji TV anime production staff in 1989 to 1992, on loan from Studio Deen and Kitty Films archives.
3. **Transformation Sequence Experience** — an immersive projection zone showing the cold-water and hot-water transformation scenes on large-format loop, synced to original Fuji TV anime audio.
4. **Rival and Fiance Lineup Panel Wall** — life-size standees and detailed character boards for Shampoo, Ukyo, Kodachi, Ryoga, Mousse, and Kuno with their iconic weapons and signature gags.
5. **MAPPA Remake Special Corner** — a late-section zone featuring the 2024 MAPPA remake key visuals alongside Season 1 highlight reels, connecting the original TV anime to the current Netflix series.
The exit funnels directly into the exhibition-exclusive merchandise shop. Expect 40 to 60 original pieces specific to the exhibition, with one-time exhibition-logo T-shirts, acrylic keychains, and a hardcover exhibition catalog retailing around 3,000 yen.

## FAQ: Ranma 1/2 Japan 2026

### Do the exhibition and the Tree Village pop-up sell the same merchandise?
No. The Ikebukuro Sunshine City Exhibition has its own exhibition-exclusive merchandise line (logo T-shirts, hardcover catalog, acrylic keychains, 3D standee via bonus ticket). The Tree Village Flower Bouquet pop-up has a separate merch line using newly drawn bouquet illustrations, including acrylic stands, canvas boards, tote bags, and postcards. Zero overlap between the two lines.

### Can I enter the Tree Village cafe without buying merch?
Tree Village pop-up cafe seats require a food or drink order (one per seat minimum, typically 1,200 to 1,800 yen). The store section is free entry and walk-through. You can shop the merchandise without ordering at the cafe.

### Is the 5,000 yen bonus ticket for the Sunshine City exhibition worth it over the 2,000 yen general?
If you collect exhibition-exclusive merchandise, yes — the 3D acrylic standee is not sold separately. If you are going for the exhibition experience only and not collecting acrylic stands, the 2,000 yen general ticket covers the same exhibit content.

### Will the Tree Village pop-up run a separate Osaka and Hakata menu?
The Tokyo leg's cafe menu will debut first. The Osaka and Hakata June 20 to July 2 legs are expected to run the same cafe menu based on the operator's 2025 Ranma "Hyakki Yagyo" pop-up pattern (same menu across all three cities, subject to regional ingredient swaps). Tree Village will confirm the menu on tree-village.jp closer to each opening date.

### I only have one day in Tokyo. Which event should I pick?
**Before May 2:** the Ikebukuro Sunshine City Exhibition is the only Ranma 2026 event open. **May 2 to 12:** either works, but the exhibition offers more content per hour and the standee ticket bonus if you're a collector. **May 13 onward:** the exhibition has closed, so Tree Village Tokyo Solamachi through May 29 is the sole Tokyo choice until June 20 when Osaka and Hakata open.
