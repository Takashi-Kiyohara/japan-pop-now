## What Is Wonder Festival?

Wonder Festival (ワンダーフェスティバル) is a biannual figure and garage kit event at Makuhari Messe in Chiba. Running since 1984, it happens twice yearly: Winter (February) and Summer (July).

The event has two halves:
- **Dealer Hall:** Individual artists sell original garage kits — handmade resin figures, often unpainted, produced in limited quantities of 10–200 pieces
- **Corporate Zone:** Major manufacturers (Good Smile Company, Kotobukiya, Max Factory, Bandai) display upcoming commercial releases and exclusive event-limited figures

WonFes 2026 Summer features: Hiroshi Yokoyama Exhibition 2026 (Maschinen Krieger) and Imaginary Creatures WonderFes (original monster & yokai-themed works).

## What's the Best Strategy for Buying at WonderFes?

**Bring cash.** Most amateur booths are cash-only. Budget ¥30,000–100,000+ (~$200–680) depending on collecting intensity. ATMs at Kaihin-Makuhari Station handle international cards.

**Arrive early.** Gates open 10:00 AM, but lines form 7:00–8:00 AM. Popular amateur circles sell out limited-run garage kits in 1–2 hours. If you want specific artists, identify their booth numbers from the catalog (published on WonFes website) and head there immediately at opening.

**Buy the catalog.** Official WonFes catalog (~¥2,500) lists every dealer with booth numbers and sample photos. Study it in line to plan your route. Digital previews go up online before the event.

**Priority: dealer hall first, corporate zone later.** Amateur one-of-a-kind pieces are time-sensitive. Corporate booths have larger stock and don't sell out the same way.

**Bring a bag and packing materials.** Garage kits are fragile resin. Bring sturdy bag, bubble wrap, and newspaper to cushion purchases for train rides home.

## How Do You Ship Figures Home?

**EMS (Express Mail Service):** Ship from any post office. Delivery in 3–5 business days to most countries. Typical box of figures runs ¥3,000–8,000 (~$20–55) internationally. Tracking included.

**Yamato Takkyubin:** Ship from any convenience store. International service available. Slightly cheaper than EMS for heavier packages.

**Pack in luggage:** Cheapest option. Wrap each figure individually in clothing or bubble wrap. Garage kits handle luggage well if packed tightly.

**Proxy shipping services:** Companies like ZenMarket, Japan Rabbit, and Buyee handle international shipping. Good if buying items through proxy services after the event.

## Can You Buy WonderFes Items After the Event?

**Secondhand market:** Items appear on Mercari (Japan's largest secondhand app), Yahoo! Auctions, and Mandarake within days. Prices are marked up 2–5x for popular items. Use [proxy shopping services](/articles/japan-proxy-shopping-2026/) to buy from Japanese-only platforms.

**Artist online stores:** Some dealers sell remaining stock or made-to-order versions through their websites or Booth.pm (Japanese creator marketplace). Follow favorite artists on X (Twitter) for announcements.

**Corporate exclusives:** Event-limited figures from major manufacturers occasionally get wider releases months later, but not guaranteed.

## What Other Figure Events Happen in Japan?

**Comiket:** Twice yearly at Tokyo Big Sight (summer & winter). Primarily doujinshi event, but many circles sell figures, art prints, crafts. Attendance 170,000+ per day. Free admission (wristband purchase required).

**AnimeJapan:** Annual trade show late March at Tokyo Big Sight. Major studios show upcoming releases. More accessible than Comiket. Admission ~¥2,600.

**Treasure Festa:** Garage kit event similar to WonFes but smaller, held several times yearly at various venues. Less crowded, more relaxed.
