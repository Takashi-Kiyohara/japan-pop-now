## Quick answer
If you are a **Detective Conan fan visiting Japan in spring or summer 2026**, this is one of the easiest themed cafe events to build into a real trip. **Detective Conan ‘Retro Port Town’ Cafe 2026** begins on **April 10, 2026** and expands across **eight locations nationwide**, including **Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Tokyo Solamachi, Nagoya, Osaka, Miyagi, Hokkaido, and Kanagawa**. Some cities open later, and some stay available longer, so the best location depends on both your travel dates and what kind of anime itinerary you want to build around it.[1]


## Why Detective Conan Cafe 2026 is especially useful for international visitors

A lot of anime cafes in Japan are great for locals but hard for short-term travelers because they are too small, too brief, or too limited to one city. Conan Cafe 2026 is different for two reasons.

First, it is a **multi-city event**. That gives overseas visitors flexibility. You do not have to force your entire schedule around one Tokyo-only venue.

Second, it connects naturally to other Conan experiences. In 2026, Conan demand is not just about one cafe stop. It also overlaps with exhibition content, movie-related buzz, and larger franchise travel planning. That makes this event valuable not only as a meal or merch stop, but as part of a broader **Conan in Japan** itinerary.

## Which cities host Detective Conan Cafe 2026?
According to event reporting, the cafe will be held at multiple **BOX cafe and space** locations in **Shibuya**, **Ikebukuro**, **Tokyo Solamachi**, plus sites in **Kanagawa, Nagoya, Osaka, Miyagi, and Hokkaido**.[1]

That is a major advantage for international visitors because it allows you to choose a venue based on your real route through Japan rather than chasing only the most famous Tokyo branch.


## Which Tokyo location is best?


![Conan's House and Beika Shopping Street in Hokuei, Tottori — official Detective Conan pilgrimage site](/images/articles/detective-conan-cafe-2026-japan-guide/body-wikimedia-5.webp)
*Conan's House and Beika Shopping Street, Hokuei, Tottori — a Detective Conan IP location useful for fans extending the cafe trip. Photo: Hsu Tzu-hsun / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.*

If your trip includes Tokyo, the most practical comparison is between **Shibuya**, **Ikebukuro**, and **Tokyo Solamachi**.

| Location | Best for | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shibuya | Travelers already staying in central Tokyo | Easy to combine with shopping, youth culture, and a faster city itinerary |
| Ikebukuro | Anime-focused travelers | Best if you want to pair the cafe with Animate, themed stores, and collab cafe hopping |
| Tokyo Solamachi | Travelers mixing anime with sightseeing | Useful if your trip already includes Tokyo Skytree or east Tokyo plans |

None of these is universally the “best” branch. The right choice depends on whether your trip is more **anime-district focused**, **general Tokyo sightseeing**, or **short-stay efficient**.


## Is Osaka a better pick than Tokyo?
Sometimes, yes.

If you are already visiting Kansai, the Osaka branch may be the smarter option because it lets you enjoy the event without adding pressure to your Tokyo schedule. This matters more in 2026 than it might have before, because current travel patterns are shifting toward **regional and longer stays**, not only Tokyo-heavy itineraries.[2]

In other words, if your route already includes Osaka, choosing the Osaka branch can make the trip feel more balanced rather than overloading Tokyo with every anime priority.


## Important date differences between cities
One of the most useful details for travelers is that not every location runs on exactly the same schedule.

The reported timing includes the following highlights:[1]

| Location / group | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Shibuya and Osaka | Open from April 10, 2026 |
| Other locations such as Kanagawa | Some open later, including May 14 starts in certain regions |
| Shibuya and Solamachi | Run until June 28, 2026 |
| Ikebukuro and Osaka | Continue until August 2, 2026 |

This makes **Ikebukuro and Osaka especially attractive** for travelers arriving later in summer. If your Tokyo dates are later than the main spring rush, Ikebukuro can be the safer Tokyo pick.


## What is the 2026 theme?
The 2026 concept is **Retro Port Town**, with new illustrations showing characters in classic clothing inspired by a historic seaside setting.[1]

That matters because it changes not only the visuals but also the overall appeal of the menu and merchandise. This is not just a standard reprint cafe. It is positioned as a seasonal concept with a distinct atmosphere, which is exactly the kind of event that tends to perform well with both dedicated fans and casual travelers looking for something memorable.


## What can you expect from the menu and goods?

The event coverage notes that the menu changes between the **first and second halves** of the event, with featured items such as a **Wind Beef Stew Hamburger**, **Detective Mixed Fry**, and movie-linked menu items like **Chihaya Hagiwara’s Lemon Mizore Pasta** and the **Angel vs. Fallen Angel Drink**.[1]

For many travelers, this makes timing more important than usual. If you care about seasonal menu differences or specific collaboration items, you should avoid assuming that every visit window offers exactly the same food lineup.

The article also highlights exclusive benefits and merchandise, including:

- food and drink order gifts
- acrylic stands
- tin badges
- and a bow tie-shaped blanket.

That means the cafe works for both **eat-and-go visitors** and **collector-driven fans**.


## How should international visitors choose between Tokyo and Osaka?
A simple rule works well:


### Choose Tokyo if…

- you are already planning an anime-heavy Tokyo trip
- you want to combine the cafe with Ikebukuro or Shibuya shopping
- or you are using Tokyo as your main base.


### Choose Osaka if…

- your Tokyo days are already crowded
- you are building a Kansai itinerary
- or you want to connect Conan with bigger regional experiences such as **Universal Cool Japan 2026** or a broader Osaka stay.

Because cities do not all open and close at the same time, your best branch depends on travel dates, not just preference.


### Do not judge only by Tokyo popularity
International visitors often default to the most famous Tokyo location. In practice, Ikebukuro, Osaka, or even a non-Tokyo branch may be more convenient depending on your route.

## FAQ

### When does Detective Conan Cafe 2026 start?
Detective Conan Cafe 2026 begins on **April 10, 2026**.[1]


### Is Detective Conan Cafe 2026 only in Tokyo?
No. It is a **nationwide multi-location event** with venues in Tokyo and several other cities including Osaka, Nagoya, Miyagi, Hokkaido, and Kanagawa.[1]


### Which Tokyo branch is best for anime travelers?
**Ikebukuro** is usually the best choice if you want to combine the cafe with other anime stores, collab cafes, and shopping. **Shibuya** is better for a broader city itinerary, while **Tokyo Solamachi** fits sightseeing-heavy trips.


### Which branches run the longest?
Based on the currently reported schedule, **Ikebukuro and Osaka** run until **August 2, 2026**, making them especially useful for later summer travel.[1]


### Is Osaka a good choice for Conan fans?
Yes. If you are already visiting Kansai, the Osaka branch can be more efficient than forcing the event into an already busy Tokyo itinerary.
