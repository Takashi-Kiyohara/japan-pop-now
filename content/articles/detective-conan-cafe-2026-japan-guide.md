If you are a **Detective Conan fan visiting Japan in spring or summer 2026**, this is one of the easiest themed cafe events to build into a real trip. **Detective Conan 'Retro Port Town' Cafe 2026** begins on **April 10, 2026** and expands across **eight locations nationwide**, including **Shibuya, Ikebukuro, Tokyo Solamachi, Nagoya, Osaka, Miyagi, Hokkaido, and Kanagawa**. Some cities open later, and some stay available longer, so the best location depends on both your travel dates and what kind of anime itinerary you want to build around it.

## Why Detective Conan Cafe 2026 is especially useful for international visitors

![Detective Conan Cafe 2026 themed interior setup in Japan](/images/articles/detective-conan-cafe-2026-japan-guide/1.jpg)

A lot of anime cafes in Japan are great for locals but hard for short-term travelers because they are too small, too brief, or too limited to one city. Conan Cafe 2026 is different for two reasons.

First, it is a **multi-city event**. That gives overseas visitors flexibility. You do not have to force your entire schedule around one Tokyo-only venue.

Second, it connects naturally to other Conan experiences. In 2026, Conan demand is not just about one cafe stop. It also overlaps with exhibition content, movie-related buzz, and larger franchise travel planning. That makes this event valuable not only as a meal or merch stop, but as part of a broader **Conan in Japan** itinerary.

## Which cities host Detective Conan Cafe 2026?

According to event reporting, the cafe will be held at multiple **BOX cafe and space** locations in **Shibuya**, **Ikebukuro**, **Tokyo Solamachi**, plus sites in **Kanagawa, Nagoya, Osaka, Miyagi, and Hokkaido**.

That is a major advantage for international visitors because it allows you to choose a venue based on your real route through Japan rather than chasing only the most famous Tokyo branch.

## Which Tokyo location is best?

![Detective Conan Cafe Tokyo location options for 2026](/images/articles/detective-conan-cafe-2026-japan-guide/2.jpg)

If your trip includes Tokyo, the most practical comparison is between **Shibuya**, **Ikebukuro**, and **Tokyo Solamachi**.

| Location | Best for | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shibuya | Travelers already staying in central Tokyo | Easy to combine with shopping, youth culture, and a faster city itinerary |
| Ikebukuro | Anime-focused travelers | Best if you want to pair the cafe with Animate, themed stores, and collab cafe hopping |
| Tokyo Solamachi | Travelers mixing anime with sightseeing | Useful if your trip already includes Tokyo Skytree or east Tokyo plans |

None of these is universally the "best" branch. The right choice depends on whether your trip is more **anime-district focused**, **general Tokyo sightseeing**, or **short-stay efficient**.

## Is Osaka a better pick than Tokyo?

Sometimes, yes.

If you are already visiting Kansai, the Osaka branch may be the smarter option because it lets you enjoy the event without adding pressure to your Tokyo schedule. This matters more in 2026 than it might have before, because current travel patterns are shifting toward **regional and longer stays**, not only Tokyo-heavy itineraries.

In other words, if your route already includes Osaka, choosing the Osaka branch can make the trip feel more balanced rather than overloading Tokyo with every anime priority.

## Important date differences between cities

One of the most useful details for travelers is that not every location runs on exactly the same schedule.

The reported timing includes the following highlights:

| Location / group | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Shibuya and Osaka | Open from April 10, 2026 |
| Other locations such as Kanagawa | Some open later, including May 14 starts in certain regions |
| Shibuya and Solamachi | Run until June 28, 2026 |
| Ikebukuro and Osaka | Continue until August 2, 2026 |

This makes **Ikebukuro and Osaka especially attractive** for travelers arriving later in summer. If your Tokyo dates are later than the main spring rush, Ikebukuro can be the safer Tokyo pick.

## What is the 2026 theme?

The 2026 concept is **Retro Port Town**, with new illustrations showing characters in classic clothing inspired by a historic seaside setting.

That matters because it changes not only the visuals but also the overall appeal of the menu and [merchandise](/articles/anime-merch-shopping-guide-japan/). This is not just a standard reprint cafe. It is positioned as a seasonal concept with a distinct atmosphere, which is exactly the kind of event that tends to perform well with both dedicated fans and casual travelers looking for something memorable.

## How should international visitors choose between Tokyo and Osaka?

A simple rule works well:

### Choose Tokyo if…

- you want to combine the cafe with Ikebukuro or Shibuya shopping
