If you want a **Jujutsu Kaisen-themed cafe experience in Japan during spring 2026**, the strongest current option is the **Jujutsu Kaisen 5th Anniversary Cafe at Sweets Paradise**. It starts on **April 2, 2026** and runs across **nine cities**, including **Tokyo, Osaka, Hiroshima, Yokohama, Omiya, Sendai, Nagoya, Kyoto, and Fukuoka**. For international visitors, the most important detail is not only the city list, but also the fact that **entry requires advance reservation through the official Sweets Paradise smartphone app**.

## Which cities are included?

According to the event information, the first wave begins in **Tokyo, Osaka, and Hiroshima** from **April 2 to April 29, 2026**. A second wave in **Yokohama, Omiya, Sendai, Nagoya, Kyoto, and Fukuoka** runs from **April 11 to April 29, 2026**.

This staggered opening matters because it changes which city makes sense for your itinerary.

## Is Tokyo or Osaka the better choice?

![Tokyo and Osaka comparison for Jujutsu Kaisen Cafe 2026](/images/articles/jujutsu-kaisen-cafes-japan-2026-guide/2.jpg)

For most international visitors, **Tokyo and Osaka are the most practical branches**, but they serve slightly different trip styles.

### Choose Tokyo if…

- your trip is centered on Tokyo anime neighborhoods
- you want to combine the cafe with other seasonal collab cafes
- or you are staying only in eastern Japan

Tokyo works especially well if your schedule already includes other spring anime content and you want a dense itinerary.

### Choose Osaka if…

- your trip already includes Kansai
- you want to reduce pressure on your Tokyo days
- or you are combining cafe travel with bigger western Japan experiences

Osaka is often the smarter choice for travelers who are already considering other major attractions or a longer regional trip.

## What about Kyoto, Nagoya, or Fukuoka?

These branches are useful because they make the event more flexible for travelers who are **not** building a Tokyo-first trip.

That matters more than it sounds. Many anime travelers still default to Tokyo, but if your itinerary already includes Kyoto, Nagoya, or Fukuoka, choosing a branch there can make the whole trip feel less rushed.

In 2026, that kind of regional flexibility fits well with the broader travel shift toward **longer stays and more distributed routes**, rather than forcing every anime priority into Tokyo alone.

## Reservation rules: the most important practical detail

The strongest practical warning for overseas visitors is simple: **you cannot treat this like a walk-in casual cafe**.

The event information says that reservations are made **exclusively through the official Sweets Paradise smartphone app**.

| Rule | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Booking method | Official Sweets Paradise smartphone app only |
| Opening window | Reservations open 5 weeks before the visit date |
| Closing window | Until 8:00 PM on the previous day, subject to availability |
| Allocation | First come, first served |
| Account limit | One active reservation at a time |

For international visitors, this is a major planning issue. If you do not prepare in advance, the event can move from "high priority" to "not realistically possible" very quickly.
