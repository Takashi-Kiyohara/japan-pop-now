If you are planning an anime-focused Japan trip in 2026 and can include Osaka, **Universal Cool Japan 2026 is one of the strongest large-scale experiences of the year**. The event begins on **January 30, 2026** at **Universal Studios Japan**, with different franchises running on different schedules. The lineup includes **Detective Conan**, **JUJUTSU KAISEN**, **Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End**, **Monster Hunter Wilds**, and the **Masquerade Series**, which makes it useful both for mainstream anime fans and for travelers who want something bigger than a standard themed cafe or exhibition.[1]

## Why Universal Cool Japan 2026 stands out

![Why Universal Cool Japan 2026 stands out — Universal Cool Japan 2026 Guide](/images/articles/universal-cool-japan-2026-guide/1.jpg)

For international visitors, the biggest advantage of Universal Cool Japan is not just that it is themed. It turns anime and Japanese entertainment brands into **park-scale experiences**: 4-D attractions, story rides, immersive theater, mystery restaurant experiences, and themed dining.[1]

That creates a very different travel value compared with Tokyo’s usual anime options. A collab cafe works well if you want a short themed stop. A temporary exhibition works well if you care deeply about one series. But Universal Cool Japan is better if you want a **major half-day or full-day anchor experience** that feels more like a destination than a side activity.

It is also one of the clearest ways to connect anime travel with the broader shift toward **regional and longer-stay itineraries**. If your trip already includes Osaka, Kyoto, or Kansai more generally, it becomes a practical alternative to building everything around Tokyo alone.[2]

## Which franchises are included?

![Which franchises are included? — Universal Cool Japan 2026 Guide](/images/articles/universal-cool-japan-2026-guide/2.jpg)

According to the official USJ page, the 2026 lineup includes the following major experiences:[1]

| **Franchise / experience** | **Event period** |
| --- | --- |
| Detective Conan: The World | January 30 – June 30, 2026 |
| JUJUTSU KAISEN: The Real 4-D — Clock Tower of Recurrence | January 30 – August 18, 2026 |
| Peril at the Masquerade – Inspired by Keigo Higashino’s Masquerade Series | January 30 – June 30, 2026 |
| MONSTER HUNTER WILDS: Wudwud Feast | November 19, 2025 – May 17, 2026 |
| Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Story Walk — Echoes of Adventure | May 30, 2026 – January 11, 2027 |

This staggered schedule matters. Universal Cool Japan is not one single event where everything is always available. What you can do depends heavily on **when** you visit.

## Which experience is best for different types of travelers?
### If you want the safest globally recognizable anime pick: JUJUTSU KAISEN
JUJUTSU KAISEN is one of the easiest recommendations for overseas visitors because the franchise has a broad global fanbase and the event period is long, running through **August 18, 2026**.[1]

That longer window makes it easier to fit into spring or summer itineraries. If you only have room for one Universal Cool Japan priority and you are not traveling specifically for Conan or Frieren, this is often the safest general choice.

### If you are already building a Conan trip: Detective Conan: The World
Conan is especially strong if you are already interested in other 2026 Conan experiences, such as exhibition content or themed cafes. In that case, Universal Cool Japan becomes more than a standalone park stop. It becomes part of a broader **Conan-in-Japan itinerary**.

If that is your travel style, continue next with our **Find by Anime in Japan 2026** hub and our related Conan event coverage.

### If you want something reflective and visually distinctive: Frieren
Frieren starts later, from **May 30, 2026**, and runs all the way to **January 11, 2027**.[1] That makes it especially useful for summer, autumn, and winter travelers who may miss the earlier spring-heavy event cycle in Tokyo.

Because Frieren is positioned as a story walk experience, it is likely to appeal more to travelers who enjoy atmosphere, emotional immersion, and fantasy world-building rather than only fast ride energy.

### If you want food-based or game-adjacent appeal: Monster Hunter Wilds
Monster Hunter Wilds: Wudwud Feast is a dining-centered experience running until **May 17, 2026**.[1] It is a useful choice if your group includes gamers or visitors who enjoy themed food experiences but are less interested in queueing mainly for rides.

## Is Universal Cool Japan worth it if you are already visiting Tokyo?
Yes, but only for the right kind of traveler.

If your trip is short and you are already overloaded with Tokyo anime plans, adding Osaka only for one park day may feel too heavy. But if you are already considering Kansai, or you are following the current trend toward **longer and more regionally distributed stays**, Universal Cool Japan becomes much more attractive.[2]

In other words, the event works best when Osaka is a real part of your itinerary, not just a rushed add-on.

## How much time should you plan?

- a **full day** if USJ is one of the major highlights of your trip,
- a **priority half day plus general park time** if you only care about one or two anime experiences,
- or a **Conan- or Jujutsu-focused visit** if your trip is built around one franchise.

Because experiences are spread across multiple parts of the park, trying to rush through it without a plan can reduce the value quickly.

## Practical planning tips
### Check dates by franchise, not just by event name
The biggest mistake international visitors can make is seeing “Universal Cool Japan 2026” and assuming every attraction is active for the whole season. The official page makes clear that periods differ by franchise.[1]

### Build Osaka around it, not after it
If you are serious about going, it is usually smarter to make USJ one of the anchors of your Osaka stay rather than trying to squeeze it into an already overpacked itinerary.

### Match the franchise to your travel style
If you like rides and spectacle, start with JUJUTSU KAISEN. If you are building a detective-themed anime trip, prioritize Conan. If you want a more atmospheric fantasy choice later in the year, Frieren may be the better pick.

## FAQ
### When does Universal Cool Japan 2026 start?
Universal Cool Japan 2026 begins on **January 30, 2026** at Universal Studios Japan.[1]

### Which anime are part of Universal Cool Japan 2026?
The official lineup includes **Detective Conan**, **JUJUTSU KAISEN**, **Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End**, **Monster Hunter Wilds**, and the **Masquerade Series**.[1]

### Is Universal Cool Japan 2026 in Tokyo?
No. It is held at **Universal Studios Japan in Osaka**.[1]

### Which experience lasts the longest?
From the official schedule currently visible, **Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Story Walk** runs from **May 30, 2026 to January 11, 2027**, while **JUJUTSU KAISEN** also has a long run through **August 18, 2026**.[1]

### Is Universal Cool Japan better than anime cafes in Tokyo?
They serve different purposes. Universal Cool Japan is better if you want a large-scale destination experience, while Tokyo cafes are better for shorter, more flexible anime stops.
