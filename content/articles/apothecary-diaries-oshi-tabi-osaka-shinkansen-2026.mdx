## What Is the Apothecary Diaries Oshi-Tabi Vol.3?

*Oshi-Tabi* is a recurring JR Tokai travel format that layers anime content onto Tokaido Shinkansen rides and the cities they connect ([JR Central Oshi-Tabi official portal](https://recommend.jr-central.co.jp/oshi-tabi/)). Vol.1 used Nagoya in 2024, Vol.2 used Kyoto in 2025, and Vol.3 — announced by JR Tokai on March 26, 2026, moves the story to Osaka. The in-universe framing is *Kusuriya no Futari-tabi* (薬屋のふたりたび, "The Apothecary's Pair Trip"), with Maomao and Jinshi traveling together to the real-world pharmacy-town district of Doshomachi.

The JR Tokai press release states the core premise plainly:

> 「新幹線乗車中に猫猫と壬氏によるプロローグボイスや朗読コンテンツが楽しめるほか、車内コンテンツに参加した方には特典をプレゼント」
> *While on the Shinkansen, passengers can enjoy prologue voice and reading content from Maomao and Jinshi, plus a bonus gift for onboard participants.*

There are three layers: onboard voice content tied to your Shinkansen ride, a digital stamp rally across Osaka, and a limited guidebook and merchandise line. The anime is mid-Season 2 (running April 2026) which is why JR is pushing hard on the tie-in right now — every returning fan is a warm lead for a Shinkansen ticket.

**Why this matters for a Japan trip:** the Tokaido Shinkansen is the one train line that almost every overseas visitor uses. Stacking an anime-exclusive experience on top of a transport link you already need is the closest Japan gets to a "free" bonus for otaku travelers.


## When Do the Onboard Voice Readings Change?

The onboard reading content rotates three times across the 95-day run. Each window features a different voice actor performing a different chapter, which turns the campaign into a three-trip collectable for diehards.


<div className="jpn-info-box">

| Period | Reader | Chapter | Notes |
|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| **Apr 17 – May 13, 2026** | Aoi Yuuki (Maomao) | Vol.4 "Final Episode" | Opening reading, highest demand |
| **May 14 – Jun 18, 2026** | Takeo Otsuka (Jinshi) | Vol.1 Ep.6 "Poison Taster" | Middle window, overlaps Tokyo BOX cafe |
| **Jun 19 – Jul 20, 2026** | Aoi Yuuki (Maomao) | Vol.1 Ep.7 "Branch" | Final window — overlaps Osaka BOX cafe |

</div>

Access works the same way across all three windows: board any Tokaido Shinkansen service (Nozomi, Hikari, or Kodama), open the JR Tokai "Oshi-Tabi" site on your phone over the onboard Wi-Fi, and the reading becomes available while you are in motion. The **certification test** (*nintei shiken*, 認定試験) sits next to it. 10 questions per volume pulled from Volumes 1 through 6 of the source novel, and clearing a full volume earns a digital certificate carrying an original voice message from either Aoi Yuuki or Takeo Otsuka.


<div className="jpn-tip">

<strong>Pro tip:</strong> The Tokaido Shinkansen free Wi-Fi drops out inside tunnels between Shizuoka and Nagoya. Load the Oshi-Tabi page before you leave Tokyo Station or Shinagawa, and keep the tab open, content can stream from cache for the tunnel sections.
</div>


## Where Are the Seven Osaka Stamp Rally Spots?

The digital stamp rally uses seven spots spread across central Osaka, plus an eighth location. Animate Umeda — that functions as the completion counter. All of them are within roughly a 6-km triangle between Shin-Osaka, Umeda, and Chuo-ku, so you can clear the whole set on foot plus two to three Osaka Metro rides.


<div className="jpn-info-box">

| # | Spot | Hours | Angle |
|---|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | **Shin-Osaka Station** (新大阪駅) | Shinkansen platform level | Arrival gate, first stamp of the trip |
| 2 | **Osaka City Central Public Hall** (大阪市中央公会堂, Nakanoshima) | 9:30 – 21:30 | Taisho-era red-brick, matches the Liangshi court aesthetic |
| 3 | **Lion Bridge** (ライオン橋 / Naniwabashi) | 24 hours | Stone-lion bridgeheads used in official key visuals |
| 4 | **Sukunahikona Shrine & Kusuri no Doshomachi Museum** (少彦名神社＆くすりの道修町資料館) | 8:00 – 17:00 | The real "medicine town" shrine — story core |
| 5 | **GLASSOWA** (Chuo-ku cafe-shop) | Shop 11:00 – 18:00, Cafe 11:00 – 17:00 | Natural-tea and cream-soda shop in a Meiji-era building, atmosphere fits the pharmacy theme |
| 6 | **Mint Museum** (造幣博物館, Kita-ku) | 9:00 – 16:45 | Meiji-era imperial aesthetics and historic buildings that echo the anime's court setting |
| 7 | **Tenjinbashi-suji / CAFE Osaka-Chakai** (天神橋筋商店街／CAFE大阪茶会) | 13:00 – 20:00 | Japan's longest covered shopping arcade (about 2.6 km) plus a Chinese tea room |
| 8 | **Animate Umeda** (completion counter) | 11:00 – 21:00 | Hand over the full stamp set for the bonus postcard |

</div>

The collabo-cafe press page describes the reward mechanic directly:

> 「スタンプラリーをコンプリートした方には描き下ろしコラボイラストを使用したオリジナルポストカードをどちらか1枚ランダムでプレゼント！」
> *Completers receive one original postcard, randomly chosen from two newly drawn collaboration illustrations.*

**My friends and I do the loop in this order:** #1 Shin-Osaka → #4 Sukunahikona (Midosuji Line to Kitahama, 7-minute walk) → #5 GLASSOWA → #3 Lion Bridge → #2 Central Public Hall → #6 Mint Museum → #7 Tenjinbashi-suji → finish at #8 Animate Umeda. This keeps you moving with the sun and avoids backtracking. Budget roughly 5 hours with short stops.

## How Do I Get the Limited 900-Yen Guidebook at Animate Umeda?

The campaign's physical anchor is an official guidebook sold only at **Animate Umeda** (NU Chayamachi 3F, about 1 minute from Hankyu Osaka-Umeda Station's Chayamachi exit, [store page](https://www.animate.co.jp/shop/umeda/)). The price per the collabo-cafe listing is **¥900 tax included**. Quantity is limited and Animate does not accept preorders for campaign goods, so stock moves fast in the first two weekends of any Oshi-Tabi run.

Beyond the guidebook, Animate Umeda is also the stamp rally completion counter and the only sales point for the new Vol.3 original merchandise line. Expect the typical Animate collab-goods range — badges, acrylic stands, random-packed trading items, with exact prices landing on the official page over April and May 2026.

## How Should I Plan a One-Day Run from Tokyo?

A same-day Tokyo round trip is the sweet spot, you ride the Shinkansen both ways (onboard content twice), clear Osaka stamps in a half-day, and don't need an extra hotel night.

My baseline itinerary:

1. **06:45 Tokyo Station** — pick up Nozomi ticket via Smart EX or JR Pass counter.
2. **07:00 Tokyo → 09:30 Shin-Osaka**, about 2h30, load the Oshi-Tabi site before departure and listen to the Aoi Yuuki reading somewhere between Shinagawa and Nagoya.
3. **09:30 Stamp #1 at Shin-Osaka platform.** Metro Midosuji to Kitahama.
4. **10:00 – 12:30** — stamps #4, #5, #3, #2 on foot between Doshomachi and Nakanoshima. Coffee at GLASSOWA.
5. **12:30 lunch**, okonomiyaki near Nakanoshima or Ajinoya if you detour to Namba.
6. **13:30 – 15:30** — stamp #6 (Mint Museum) and stamp #7 (Tenjinbashi-suji arcade).
7. **16:00 – 17:30**. Animate Umeda for the completion postcard and the ¥900 guidebook.
8. **18:00 Shin-Osaka → 20:30 Tokyo**, second onboard reading window, certification quiz on the way home.

Total Shinkansen spend: roughly **¥27,740 non-reserved or ¥29,440 reserved** round trip Tokyo–Shin-Osaka (Nozomi not available on a standard JR Pass — use Smart EX or a regular ticket, or add the Nozomi-option upgrade to the JR Pass). Food plus stamps plus guidebook lands around ¥5,000. One day, one IC card, one set of memories.

## Why this hits with fans

The real brief behind Oshi-Tabi is not just "anime tourism", it is a softer sell on *seichi junrei* (聖地巡礼, literal-pilgrimage to anime locations) mixed with JR Tokai's long-running "Discover Japan" heritage campaigns. Doshomachi, the pharmacy district in central Osaka, has been the heart of Japanese traditional medicine for 400 years, and the Sukunahikona Shrine there is considered the national patron shrine of pharmacy. Placing Maomao, a poison-taster with encyclopedic herb knowledge, into that real neighborhood lets Japanese fans visit a working shrine that their grandparents already knew about, but with an anime overlay that makes it feel current.

The Shinkansen layer adds another emotional beat. For most Japanese, the Tokaido Shinkansen is commute-level boring by adult life. Turning it into "the train where Maomao reads you a chapter" is a deliberate reframing — it re-enchants the most-used line in the country. Fans repost clips of "the moment the reading starts at Shinagawa" on X the week each new rotation opens, and domestic travel blogs have historically ranked well for "Oshi-Tabi Nagoya review" and "Oshi-Tabi Kyoto review" because Japanese readers treat each volume as a seasonal checklist.


## FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a JR Pass or can I buy a normal Shinkansen ticket?**

A regular ticket works. Tokyo to Shin-Osaka is about ¥13,870 one way non-reserved on the Nozomi, or ¥14,720 reserved. The JR Pass is only worth it if you plan two or more long-distance Shinkansen legs during the trip, this Osaka day alone does not justify the pass. For Smart EX English booking, registration takes about 10 minutes and you only need a credit card and a passport photo page.

**Q: Is the onboard voice content in Japanese only?**

Yes. The prologue and reading content are performed in Japanese by the original voice actors. The Oshi-Tabi site has an English UI toggle for navigation, but the audio is not dubbed or subtitled. A Japanese reading-app like Yomiwa or Google Lens translating a screenshot of the certification quiz helps; the quiz questions are multiple-choice with visual clues.

**Q: Can I do the stamp rally without riding the Shinkansen?**

Technically yes — the seven Osaka stamps are accessible from Shin-Osaka on foot and by metro. But half the point of Oshi-Tabi is the onboard voice content plus the certification quiz, and those are tied to Tokaido Shinkansen rides. If you are already in Osaka, at minimum ride one round-trip stop (Shin-Osaka to Kyoto is about 15 minutes and ¥1,420) to trigger the onboard layer.

**Q: What if the guidebook at Animate Umeda is sold out?**

In past Oshi-Tabi runs, Animate has restocked campaign goods roughly weekly during peak periods. Check Animate Umeda's official page on animate.co.jp or their X account for same-day stock updates. Resellers typically charge 2 to 3 times face value for the guidebook if you have to go that route, steep, but workable if you want the collectible.

**Q: How long does the full trip actually take end-to-end?**

From Tokyo Station doors to Tokyo Station doors: roughly 13 hours if you take the 7:00 out and 18:00 back. Cut it to 9 hours if you fly from Haneda to Itami, but then you lose the onboard reading and the certification quiz, which is half of what you came for. For first-time overseas visitors, the full Shinkansen round-trip is the correct answer.
