## What Is DARK MOON and Why the Japan Hype?

DARK MOON: The Blood Altar (다크문: 혈제) is a Naver Webtoon thriller written and illustrated by HYBE, launched 2021 with seven clan-leader leads built around the seven ENHYPEN members. The series ran 70 episodes, 126 million reads, and its 24-episode Troyca anime adaptation aired on Japanese TV from January 13 to late March 2026, pushing Japan search for the IP to an all-time high in Q1. Each clan leader is designed around a specific ENHYPEN member, and HYBE licensed the anime OST to the group.

<div className="jpn-info-box"><strong>Entity note:</strong> THE Chara CAFE (ザ キャラ カフェ) is a Tokyo collab-cafe chain with three Ikebukuro-area branches. The Grandscape Ikebukuro 2F store, opened 2024, is where DARK MOON is running — not the Kyu Plaza Ikebukuro branch or the B1F second branch in the same building.</div>

## How Do I Book From Overseas?

THE Chara CAFE runs on LivePocket (Play Tickets), the same service Lawson and many Tokyo collab cafes use. Slots are 90-minute seated blocks, about 12 seatings a day starting 11:00 AM, last one around 7:00 PM.

**Booking URL:** livepocket.jp — search "DARK MOON THE Chara CAFE" once slots open, typically 1 to 2 weeks before collab start.

1. **Create a LivePocket account** — email and password only. No Japanese mobile required.
2. **Buy the seat reservation** — usually a 700 yen fee credited back as in-store discount.
3. **Receive the QR code** — stored under "My Tickets." Show it at the 2F entrance.
4. **Arrive 10 minutes early** — seating rotates exactly on the 90-minute mark.

<div className="jpn-tip"><strong>Pro tip:</strong> Golden Week dates (April 29, May 3-6) sell out within minutes of release. Target weekdays April 28, 30, or May 1 for the easiest slot.</div>

## What Is On the Menu and What About the Random Bromides?

The collab wraps each of the seven clan leaders around a food or drink. The anchor pasta is **Heli's Special Meat Sauce Pasta** (ヘリ特製ミートソースパスタ) — Heli is the protagonist and ENHYPEN Jungwon analog. Themed-color drinks cover Ian, Jino, Solon, Shion, Jaka, and Noah, plus hamburger steak and fried-rice mains in the standard THE Chara CAFE format.

**Random draw math:** one item equals one random draw. Want Heli specifically? Two drinks plus two food items across two visits stacks four draws, roughly 52 percent chance of at least one Heli. Or buy the postcard set (770 yen, all seven).

## Your 1-Hour Ikebukuro K-Pop Walk-Around

Ikebukuro's East exit is the densest K-pop retail cluster in Tokyo outside Shin-Okubo, and every stop below sits within a 12-minute walk of Grandscape 2F.

1. **Animate Ikebukuro Main Store** (9-min walk): 9-floor flagship. 7F for webtoon merch, 1F for event random-bromide gacha.
2. **Otome Road Ikebukuro** (8-min walk): K-Books, Mandarake, Animate Girls. K-Books's K-pop corner stocks Naver Webtoon print volumes and used ENHYPEN photocards at half Korea-primary price.
3. **Ikebukuro PARCO** (6-min walk): rotating basement pop-ups, check PARCO 8F schedule the morning you visit.

A realistic sequence after an 11 AM or 2 PM cafe seating: Grandscape 2F → PARCO 8F (6 min walk + 10 min shop) → Otome Road K-Books (8 min walk + 15 min shop) → Ikebukuro East exit (7 min walk). Total 1 hour 16 minutes including the cafe seating.

## Why Japanese Fans Love This Collab

THE Chara CAFE's market position is specifically as the Korean and webtoon collab cafe — the venue has hosted Mo Dao Zu Shi, Heaven Official's Blessing, and multiple BL Korean-webtoon collabs over three years. For DARK MOON fans the pull is threefold: explicit ENHYPEN-member character mapping, a top-tier Troyca anime Japanese dub, and the cafe-staff uniform illustration style that makes the collab emotionally specific rather than generic. It is also the first domestic-Japan venue to sell Heli-specific physical goods — Naver Webtoon merchandise ships internationally but rarely reaches Japanese brick-and-mortar.

## FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a reservation, or can I walk in?

For the seated cafe experience, reservation is effectively mandatory during this 12-day collab. THE Chara CAFE sells seats on LivePocket in 90-minute blocks with around 12 seatings per day. Golden Week (April 29, May 3-6) will sell out within minutes of release. Walk-ups get directed to the standing goods-only line, no seat needed.

### Is English supported at the cafe?

Not formally. Menus are Japanese with photos, staff speak limited English, LivePocket is Japanese-only. Bring Google Translate's camera mode and a credit card that works on Japanese readers. Pointing at goods display cases works fine.

### Can I buy more than one of the same goods item?

Yes for chooseable items (acrylic keychains, figures, postcard sets), no official cap. For random-draw items (clear cards, collection cards, acrylic blocks, coasters, bromides), there is a 4 to 6 draw cap per transaction to stop one buyer clearing a type. Confirm the exact cap at the goods register.

### Can I buy K-pop ENHYPEN goods inside the cafe?

No. The cafe sells THE Chara CAFE DARK MOON webtoon and anime illustrations only. For ENHYPEN lightsticks, photocards, or albums, walk 8 minutes to K-Books Ikebukuro or Animate Ikebukuro.

### Is a next DARK MOON cafe collab confirmed after May 6?

Not as of the April 21 announcement. The Gray City sequel webtoon is still running and HYBE has not confirmed an anime adaptation yet. The April 25 to May 6 THE Chara CAFE run is currently the only Japan-side in-person DARK MOON collab announced for 2026.
