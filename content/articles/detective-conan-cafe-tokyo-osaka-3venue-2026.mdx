## What Is the 2026 Retro Port Town Cafe?

This is a **licensed pop-up** — BOX cafe&space runs the kitchen and handles reservations; Shogakukan and Yomiuri TV approve the menu plating and character artwork. The theme "Retro Port Town" (港町レトロ) nods to *The Fallen Angel of the Highway*, where several scenes play out in an old-school yoshoku diner in a seaside town. Every character illustration shows a period-matched outfit, and the dishes are all showa-era Japanese western food — hamburg steak, napolitan spaghetti, curry rice, pudding à la mode.

**The anniversary backdrop matters.** Detective Conan began broadcasting on Yomiuri TV on January 8, 1996, so 2026 is the 30th year of the anime. The main anniversary event is the touring *30th Anniversary TV Anime Exhibition* that opened at Tokyo Dome City Prism Hall on February 20, 2026 and tours Osaka, Fukuoka, and Hiroshima through 2026–2027 — a separate ticketed exhibition, not a cafe. The Retro Port Town Cafe covered here is the movie-tied collaboration that runs in parallel across spring and summer.

The [official Detective Conan Cafe 2026 site](https://conan-cafe.jp/) is the canonical source for dates, menu updates, and the reservation link. [Collabo-cafe.com's event page](https://collabo-cafe.com/events/collabo/conan-movie-vol29-cafe-2026/) mirrors the menu and merchandise in full and is easier to machine-translate in Chrome.

## Phase 1 vs Phase 2 — Why the Dates Matter

The event splits into two phases with separate menus and novelty gifts — if you have a favourite item, the dates are the gating factor.

- **Phase 1 (前期):** April 10 to May 17, 2026 — covers the "Wind's" Beef Stew Hamburg, White Feather Napolitan, Fallen Angel Rice Curry, Angel Pudding à la Mode
- **Closed:** May 18–19 for phase changeover (some venues also close May 21)
- **Phase 2 (後期):** May 20 onward — through June 28 in Shibuya, July 5 in Nagoya, August 2 in Osaka

Phase 2 swaps the three phase-1-only dishes for new ones and rotates the coaster and card illustrations. Exact Phase 2 menu items were not public as of April 2026 — follow [@conan_cafe on X](https://x.com/conan_cafe) or the official site for the mid-May menu drop.

## How Do I Book? The 715 vs 3,850 Yen Reservation Tiers

This is where overseas fans get stuck — the cafe runs a **paid reservation system** rather than a free lottery. You pay the slot fee when booking, and the slot fee is in addition to what you order once seated.

**Standard reservation: 715 yen per person (tax included)**
- One random invitation card, priority entry to your 75-minute seating
- No merchandise included — you buy goods separately in the same building

**Premium reservation: 3,850 yen per person (tax included)**
- Acrylic keychain (random), character pouch, puzzle book, and shopping bag included
- Priority merchandise access before general sale
- The goods bundle retails around 4,500 yen piecewise, so this tier saves money if you want the keychain

**How the booking calendar works:**
- Member + public lotteries ran February 25 to March 11, 2026 (both closed)
- **Rolling cancellation release:** From April 10 onward, cancelled slots go back on the official site as first-come-first-served — this is the main way overseas visitors secure seats in 2026
- Payment by credit card (Visa / Mastercard / JCB / Amex) or Japanese convenience store

<div className="jpn-warning">
<strong>Heads up:</strong> The reservation site is Japanese only, and children aged 4 and older need their own reservation. If you cannot navigate the booking flow, the nuclear option is to have your hotel concierge book on your behalf — many Shibuya and Umeda-area hotels have done this for overseas guests before and will accept a print-out of the page.
</div>

**The booking strategy that worked for me:** Set a timer for Japan Standard Time 10:00 AM weekday mornings from late April onward. Cancellations post in batches Monday / Wednesday / Friday. Refresh the date you want and book the earliest open weekday lunch — evenings and Saturdays are gone within minutes.

## The Puzzle Reward Mechanic (Why Regulars Visit Three Times)

Every reservation ships with a one-page puzzle card — a visual cipher tied to that phase's illustrated characters. Complete the puzzle at the cafe and staff stamp your loyalty sheet. **Three stamps in Phase 2 across any combination of the eight venues earn a clear-case Conan file folder** not sold as merch. Phase 1 had a simpler one-puzzle-one-sticker reward that regulars already chased in April.

For overseas visitors on a two-week trip, one puzzle stamp in Tokyo is still a clean memento even if the three-stamp clear case is out of reach.

## Why This Cafe Hits Different

The Japanese response has been quieter than the 2024 30th Anniversary Cafe but steadier — the film is playing well in its third release week, and Phase 1 slots have held around 80% booked. Regulars call the Retro Port Town theme a "reprieve" from Conan's usual bright-palette collab aesthetic — muted browns, period-costume illustrations, and yoshoku menu all lean nostalgic.

The three metro venues are well chosen for a film-year tour. Each sits within walking distance of a major cinema chain, so fans can catch the film and the cafe in the same half-day.
