If you are visiting Tokyo in April 2026 and want a **My Hero Academia-themed cafe stop that fits naturally into a wider anime day**, the strongest current choice is the **My Hero Academia 'Diner' themed pop-up cafe at DECOTTO by animate cafe in Ikebukuro**. The event runs from **April 3 to April 26, 2026**, and works especially well for international visitors because it sits in one of Tokyo's easiest districts for anime shopping, cafe hopping, and character goods browsing.

## Why this My Hero Academia cafe works so well for travelers

![My Hero Academia Cafe themed interior setup in Ikebukuro](/images/articles/my-hero-academia-cafe-tokyo-2026/1.jpg)

A lot of anime cafes in Tokyo are appealing in isolation but awkward in real travel terms. They may be in less convenient neighborhoods, last for only a short time, or require a dedicated trip with little else nearby.

This one is different because it is in **Ikebukuro**.

For international visitors, that changes everything. Ikebukuro is one of the best districts in Tokyo for turning a single cafe booking into a **half-day anime plan**. You can pair a timed cafe visit with Animate, character-goods shopping, other seasonal collab cafes, and general department-store browsing without needing complicated transport.

That makes this event much more practical than a standalone "go for the photo and leave" stop.

## What is the theme of the 2026 cafe?

The 2026 concept is **Waffle Sandwich Diner**, a playful food-focused theme built around sweet and savoury waffles, tea, and coffee.

That matters because it gives the event a stronger identity than a generic collaboration menu. It is visually distinctive, easy to recognize in photos, and practical for travelers who want a cafe experience that feels like a real stop rather than a pure merchandise counter.

The featured art also shows **U.A. High School Class 1-A** characters in colorful diner-inspired outfits, which strengthens the event for fans who care about exclusive illustrations and photo appeal.

## Why Ikebukuro is the best part of this event

## What can you expect at the cafe?

According to event coverage, the cafe offers:

- sweet and savoury waffle menu items
- tea and coffee options
- one random coaster from **13 designs** for each food or drink ordered
- original merchandise using the new diner-themed illustrations
- and additional collectible goods such as keychains, badges, and clear rubber coasters

The product lineup reportedly also includes a **motif jacket inspired by Katsuki Bakugo**, which makes the event relevant not only for casual cafe-goers but also for more merchandise-focused fans.
