## What Is the Kizuna Collaboration at ufotable Cafe?

This is a studio-run event, not a licensed pop-up. ufotable animates Demon Slayer frame by frame, and the same team designs the cafe's plates, drink colors, and wall displays. The "Kizuna" theme — written 絆 in Japanese and meaning "bonds" or "ties between people" — reflects the character pair drinks that anchor the menu. Every drink on the menu represents a relationship: Tanjiro and his sister Nezuko, Tanjiro and his rival Giyu, Tanjiro and his master Urokodaki, and so on.

**The rerun context matters.** Fuji TV started airing the full Demon Slayer TV series again on Sunday mornings from March 31, 2026, the 9:30 AM family slot. The cafe menu is split to match — the first half from March 31 to April 20 covers the opening Tanjiro arc, and the second half from April 21 to May 6 shifts to the Final Selection and Urokodaki training sequences. If you want a specific character drink, check the dates before you apply.

The [official ufotable Cafe collaboration page](https://www.ufotable.co.jp/cafe/collaboration/kimetu/) is the canonical source for dates and phase splits. [Collabo-cafe.com's event page](https://collabo-cafe.com/events/collabo/kimetsu-rerun-vol1-ufotable-cafe-2026/) mirrors the dates and lists the merchandise lineup in full.

### Pair Drinks (all 650 yen each)

The drinks are where the "Kizuna" theme lives. Every drink represents a character pair. 2 drinks run the full event; 3 are first-half only; 3 are second-half only.

**Full run (March 31 to May 6):** Tanjiro and Nezuko (milk + strawberry syrup + matcha, cold) and Tanjiro and Giyu (blue curacao + tonic + cola syrup, cold).

**First half only (March 31 to April 20):** Tanjiro and Urokodaki (green tea + manju + ramune, hot), Tanjiro and Sabito (green tea + manju + marmalade, hot), Tanjiro and Makomo (green tea + manju + strawberry jam, hot).

**Second half only (April 21 to May 6):** Tanjiro and Zenitsu (muscat jelly + melon syrup + vitamin soda, cold), Tanjiro and Genya (grape juice + tonic + matcha, cold), Tanjiro and Kanao (green tea + purple sweet potato manju + edible flowers, hot).

## How Do I Book a Lottery Reservation?

This is the part overseas visitors get stuck on. The 3 lottery venues (Tokyo, Nagoya, Osaka) use a two-tier weekly system. The first tier runs on LivePocket, a Japanese electronic ticket platform; the second tier runs on the ufotable reservation portal for cancellations. Both are in Japanese.

**Step 1 — Primary lottery (every week):**
- Applications open **every Thursday at 6:00 PM JST**
- Application window closes **Thursday at 11:59 PM JST** the same day — only 6 hours
- Winners are announced **Friday around noon** by email
- Maximum **2 guests per reservation**

**Step 2 — Secondary first-come booking (cancellations only):**
- Cancellation slots are announced on each cafe's X account around **Monday at 4:00 PM JST**
- Booking opens **Monday at 8:00 PM JST**, first come, first served
- Saturday and Sunday slots sell out within 60 seconds

**Lawson Ticket fallback:** Some ufotable collaboration phases also open a Lawson Ticket allocation that you redeem on a Loppi machine in any Lawson convenience store. When Lawson Ticket is active for a phase, the URL appears on the official ufotable cafe page. If you cannot read Japanese, our [Lawson Ticket and Loppi guide](/articles/lawson-ticket-anime-cafe-booking) walks through the screen-by-screen Loppi flow in English, including the kanji prompts you need to memorize.

<div className="jpn-warning">
<strong>Heads up:</strong> LivePocket and the ufotable reservation portal both require a Japanese email address or social login for some steps, and the payment screen is Japanese only. If you cannot navigate the system, your backup plan is to visit Tokushima or Kitakyushu — both are walk-in, no reservation needed. Some hotels near Nogata Station are familiar with the LivePocket lottery and will submit on your behalf if you ask 48 hours in advance.
</div>

**The booking strategy that worked for me:** Set a phone alarm for 5:55 PM JST every Thursday. Pre-load the LivePocket page with your account logged in. The moment 6:00 PM JST hits, submit for a weekday lunch slot — those have the lowest competition. Saturday evening slots fill within the first 2 minutes of the lottery window.

## Why Japanese People Love This

The rebroadcast is not just reruns in Japan — it is a cultural event. Demon Slayer built its audience gradually from 2019 through word of mouth, then exploded with the Mugen Train film that grossed 40.4 billion yen and created millions of fans who never saw the original broadcast in real time. The 2026 full rebroadcast on Fuji TV's Sunday morning family slot brings the entire franchise back into the weekly conversation, and the ufotable Cafe menu shifts every few weeks to match.

Japanese regulars treat each Sunday broadcast as a shared moment — they live-tweet reactions, then visit the ufotable Cafe the same week to eat the menu item that matches the episode. The "Kizuna" theme explicitly celebrates the connections between the characters, and by extension the connections between the fans who keep returning. Many regulars plan 3 or 4 visits across the 5-week run to catch every pair drink — something the lottery system accommodates by releasing fresh slots every Thursday.
