## SHOOTING SPECIFICATIONS

### Featured Image (アイキャッチ)
- **Dimensions**: 1200 × 630px (16:9 ratio)
- **Format**: JPG (quality: 85%), WebP (quality: 80%)
- **Purpose**: Hero/banner shot — stop scroll, convey article topic at a glance
- **Style**: Bright, focused, anime-adjacent (not manga panels)

### Body Images
- **Dimensions**: 800 × 450px minimum (16:9 preferred)
- **Count**: 2–4 per article (marked as **[1/2/3/4]**)
- **Placement**: After intro, mid-article, near key info sections
- **Style**: Clear wide shots (location/storefront), detail shots (signage/merch), medium shots (crowds/vibe)

### General Rules
- **Lighting**: Natural daylight preferred; avoid harsh shadows on signs/merchandise
- **People**: Avoid faces (privacy); crowd shots OK if blurred/distant
- **Weather**: Overcast is fine; heavy rain/fog = reschedule
- **Seasons**: Account for sakura (late March–early April), leaves (October–November)

## PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

### Pre-Shoot
- [ ] Check article topic and theme carefully
- [ ] Scout location if unfamiliar
- [ ] Confirm opening hours (cafes close mid-afternoon; event hours vary)
- [ ] Check weather forecast (aim for overcast or morning light)
- [ ] Bring backup batteries and memory cards

### On Location
- [ ] Shoot featured image first (hero wide shot)
- [ ] Get 3–4 detailed/supporting shots
- [ ] Vary focal length (wide for context, medium for detail, close for signage)
- [ ] Aim for natural lighting (avoid harsh noon shadows)
- [ ] Check for people in frame and respect privacy

### Post-Shoot
- [ ] Rename files by article slug: `[article-slug]_01.jpg`, `[article-slug]_02.jpg`, etc.
- [ ] Resize to spec before uploading
- [ ] Remove metadata if sensitive location info present
- [ ] Store originals in backup folder

## NOTES

- **Collab Cafe Timing**: Cafes usually close by 7 PM; shoot early afternoon for best light
- **Event Seasons**: AnimeJapan (March), Comiket (December/August), Wonder Festival (February/August)
- **Pilgrimage Locations**: Visit weekday mornings for fewer crowds in background
- **Shopping Districts**: Weekday afternoons (1 PM–4 PM) have lighter foot traffic than weekends
- **Weather Safety**: Reschedule if heavy rain/fog obscures signage or landmark details
- **Privacy**: Always blur/distance crowds; no face close-ups of shoppers or attendees
- **Consistency**: Match tone and lighting across article series (e.g., all cafe articles shot in similar light)
