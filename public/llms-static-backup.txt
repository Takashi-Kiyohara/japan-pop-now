# Japan Pop Now
> Your ultimate English-language guide to Japan's anime and pop culture scene — collab cafes, pilgrimage spots, area guides, and travel tips for international visitors.

## About
Japan Pop Now is a specialized travel-culture media site for international anime fans visiting Japan. We cover everything from booking anime collaboration cafes to finding real-life anime locations, navigating otaku districts, and practical travel logistics.

## Content Categories
- **Collab Cafes & Events**: How to book, menus, current schedules for anime collaboration cafes across Japan (Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya). Updated monthly.
- **Anime Pilgrimage (Seichi Junrei)**: Real-life filming/inspiration locations from popular anime — Slam Dunk (Kamakura), Your Name (Shinjuku), Jujutsu Kaisen (Shibuya), and 20+ series.
- **Area Guides**: Neighborhood-level guides for anime fans — Akihabara, Ikebukuro, Nakano Broadway, Den Den Town (Osaka), plus hidden spots.
- **Travel Tips**: Practical guides — Japan Rail Pass, eSIM/SIM cards, luggage forwarding (takkyubin), airport transfers, budget tips.
