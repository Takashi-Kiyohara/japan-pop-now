# JNTO (Japan National Tourism Organization) — Press/Media Contact

**Tier**: 1 (70%+ yield expected — free press kits explicitly offered)
**Last verified**: 2026-04-22

## Contact Method

**Media Inquiry Form (press only, gated):**
https://www.jnto.go.jp/en/media/inquiry/

Form fields (required fields vary): Name, title, country/region, media name and
company, country of publication, email, phone, inquiry content (max 800
characters), desired response deadline.

Verbatim policy statement on the form:

> "This form is for inquiries from members of the media only. Please note
> that inquiries from private individuals submitted through this form will
> not receive a response."

Source: https://www.jnto.go.jp/en/media/inquiry/ (fetched 2026-04-22)

## Recommended Approach

Submit via the Media Inquiry form. Japan Pop Now qualifies as media (bilingual
editorial, regular cadence). In the "inquiry content" text area, link the
specific article URL and name the type of imagery needed (anime-pilgrimage
landmark photos / Tokyo neighborhood streetscapes / Shinkansen interior /
airport arrival / etc.). The Photo & Video Library at japan.travel may
already contain usable assets — check before writing, and reference any
specific assets by URL in the inquiry.
