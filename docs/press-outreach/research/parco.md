## Recommended Approach

PARCO is central for anime collab coverage because:

- Shibuya PARCO 6F Cyberspace (gaming / anime flagships)
- Ikebukuro PARCO (multiple rotating collab events)
- Nagoya PARCO (Jujutsu Kaisen and other collab pop-ups)
- Fukuoka PARCO (regional collab events)

Request pattern:

1. Submit via /contact/ with subject line specifying "press / media
   inquiry — [PARCO店舗名] collab event image usage"
2. If PARCO corporate declines or defers, individual store PR contacts
   are sometimes listed on each store's subsite (e.g., shibuya.parco.jp,
   ikebukuro.parco.jp) — those store-level contacts are often faster
3. For THE GUEST cafe&diner at Shibuya PARCO specifically, THE GUEST
   has its own operator (Red Horse Corporation) and its own contact
   channel; PARCO corporate may redirect
