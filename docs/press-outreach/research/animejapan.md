## Recommended Approach

AnimeJapan holds formal press accreditation during the event week (usually
late March). For OFF-SEASON outreach like article coverage:

1. Load https://www.animejapan.jp/en/ and scroll the footer for Contact,
   Press, or 取材申込 links
2. If nothing is visible, the event-organizer society
   (一般社団法人アニメジャパン) publishes an archive page with a general
   inquiry form at the main AnimeJapan domain — locate it manually
3. For press-kit requests about past/upcoming editions, the simplest path
   is often emailing the event's PR agency, which is usually named in the
   bottom of the annual press release PDF. Check a recent AnimeJapan press
   release (available on the official site) for the PR agency contact
   line
