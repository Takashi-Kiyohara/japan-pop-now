## Recommended Approach

The /interview/ form expects a relatively formal media credential — "program
name, broadcast date, number of staff" is geared toward TV and print
coverage. Japan Pop Now's online-article format may generate a slower
response. Fill the form honestly:

- **media type**: Online media
- **publication name**: Japan Pop Now
- **reference URL**: https://www.japan-pop-now.com
- **publication date**: The target article's publish/update date
- **coverage subject**: [image use permission for the target article]
- **interview format**: Remote (email / form-based correspondence only)

If the request is specifically about Animate Cafe imagery, use the
Google Form at https://forms.gle/H2hPNGiSXnx9GQht5 — that's a lower-
friction inbox.
