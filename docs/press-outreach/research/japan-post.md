## Recommended Approach

1. Load https://www.japanpost.jp/ → look for 企業情報 → 広報 or ニュース
   releases → most Japan Post news releases include a 広報担当 (PR manager)
   contact line at the bottom
2. Alternative (pragmatic): for an overseas-shipping article angle,
   Japan Post's English EMS customer page
   (https://www.post.japanpost.jp/int/ems/ or similar) sometimes links
   an international-shipping inquiry team that is faster than the
   corporate PR route
3. Expect 2-3 week response time from Japan Post corporate PR; they are
   conservative with image licensing
