## Recommended Approach

Yamato is a major listed corporation (Yamato Holdings). For press/media:

1. Visit the Japanese corporate site at https://www.yamato-hd.co.jp/
   (parent holding company) — PR & IR contacts are typically under
   企業情報 → 広報 or equivalent
2. Alternative: Japan Pop Now's use case (luggage forwarding article)
   aligns more with Yamato Transport's "Business" inbound-tourism team
   than with PR. Look for an inbound-tourism or JNTO-liaison contact.
3. Third option: Yamato's TA-Q-BIN (takkyubin) service for foreign
   visitors has its own marketing arm (visible at
   https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/en/ under "Traveling to Japan"
   navigation) — asking that team for sample photos of delivery trucks
   / sorting depots / customer-facing depot counters is usually simpler
   than a cold press inquiry.
