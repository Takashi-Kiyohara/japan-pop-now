## Recommended Approach

Most Sweets Paradise anime collabs are joint projects between Sweets
Paradise and an IP holder (e.g., production committee). Sweets Paradise
typically has limited authority to clear character imagery on their own.
Two parallel tracks:

1. **Venue interior / branding shots** (store shots, non-character):
   use /contact — Sweets Paradise can clear these directly
2. **Character / IP artwork shots** (menu items with anime art, key
   visuals): Sweets Paradise cannot clear on their own. Ask /contact for
   a re-forward or introduction to the IP holder's PR contact — this
   signals good intent even if Japan Pop Now ends up in IP-holder limbo
